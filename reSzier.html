Privacy Policy for reSzier

Last updated: October 2026

🛡️ 100% On-Device Privacy: In reSzier, all photo operations (resizing, cropping, rotation, compression, format conversion, and watermarking) happen directly on your device. We never upload, collect, or store your photos on any server.

1. Overview

Welcome to reSzier. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how your information is handled when using our photo editing tools.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Your Photos & Media: All images remain strictly local on your smartphone. We do not store or transmit your media over the internet.
  • Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts to use the app.

3. Android Permissions Used

  • Photo Picker & Media Storage: Used solely to let you choose photos from your device to edit and save edited results back to your gallery or Downloads folder.
  • Internet & Network Access: Used exclusively to request and display Google AdMob advertisements.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To provide all features free of charge, reSzier displays Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded Video Ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may process non-personal diagnostic data and advertising IDs in compliance with Google Play Developer policies.

reSzier saves your local settings (such as Dark/Light theme, default format, and your active editing draft) securely in your device's isolated app storage so you can resume editing seamlessly.
