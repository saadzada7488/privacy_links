Privacy Policy for reSzier

Last updated: October 2026

🛡️ 100% On-Device Data Processing: In reSzier, all image operations (resizing, aspect ratio cropping, rotation, batch compression, format conversion, and watermarking) happen strictly on your device. We do NOT collect, upload, share, or store your photos on any external server.

1. Overview

Welcome to reSzier ("we", "our", or "the app"). We respect your privacy and are fully committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy informs you about how reSzier operates and how your data is handled when using our application.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Your Photos & Media: Your pictures remain entirely private and local to your smartphone. No images are ever transmitted over the internet to our systems or third parties.
  • Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect your name, phone number, address, contacts, or passwords.

3. Android Permissions Used

  • Photo Picker & Storage (Scoped Access): Used strictly to allow you to select images from your gallery to edit and save resized/converted photos back to your device storage.
  • Internet & Network State: Used solely to display advertisements via Google AdMob and to verify network status for ad requests.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To provide all photo resizing and editing tools free of charge, reSzier integrates Google AdMob to display Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded Video Ads. Google AdMob may use device advertising identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID) and non-personal diagnostic data to deliver compliant ads according to Google Play Policies.

For more information on Google's privacy practices, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps

5. Local Drafts & User Preferences

reSzier saves your local UI preferences (such as Dark/Light mode theme, output format preference, and active editing session draft) inside your device's isolated app storage. This allows you to resume your work if you close the app without losing your progress.

6. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

reSzier is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
