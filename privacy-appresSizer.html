Privacy Policy for Resizer

Last updated: October 2026

🔒 100% On-Device Privacy: Resizer processes all your photos and images directly on your device. We do NOT upload, collect, or store your photos on any external servers or clouds.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Resizer ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your information when you use our image resizing, cropping, converting, and editing services.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal Photos & Media: All image processing (resizing, aspect ratio cropping, rotation, compressing, and watermark removal) is executed locally on your device's memory. Your images never leave your smartphone.
  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not require accounts, logins, emails, phone numbers, or passwords to use the core features of the app.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

  • Photo Picker / Storage Access: Required solely to allow you to select images from your gallery for editing and to save processed output files back to your device's gallery or Downloads folder.
  • Internet Access & Network State: Used exclusively to serve Google AdMob advertisements and check connectivity for ad rendering.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Services

To keep the application free for everyone, we use Google AdMob to display Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded Video Ads. Third-party advertising partners may collect anonymized diagnostic data and mobile advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) in compliance with Google Play Developer Program policies.

For more details on how Google uses data from apps using its services, please visit:

  • Google Advertising Policies & Terms
  • Google Privacy Policy

5. Local Storage & Draft Persistence

The app stores local user preferences (such as Dark/Light mode, default export format, and your active editing draft) securely within private Android application storage. This ensures you can resume your editing session if the app is closed. This data remains on your physical device only.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our app is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
