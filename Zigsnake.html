Privacy Policy for Zig Snake

📅 Effective Date: October 27, 2026 🎮 Game: Zig Snake (Neon Serpent) 👤 Developer: Saad Zada 🏢 Studio: Mr Hideo 📦 Package ID: com.mrhideo.zigsnake

Welcome to Zig Snake, developed by Saad Zada under the studio and brand name Mr Hideo ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you download, install, and play the Zig Snake mobile application on Android and other platforms.

1. About Zig Snake & Core Features

Zig Snake is a high-speed, action-packed competitive snake arena game featuring:

  • Diverse Game Modes: Classic Arena, Survival Waves, Time Rush Countdown, and Speed Frenzy.
  • Custom Snake Skins & Abilities: Visual skins with active/passive power abilities (Speed Boost, Energy Shields, Fire Trails, Teleport Warps, Freezing Blasts, Gold Magnets, Stealth/Ghost Mode, and Shockwave Blasts).
  • In-Game Rewards Economy: Virtual Coins and Gems earned via gameplay performance, daily login streaks, achievement missions, and voluntary rewarded video ads. There is no real-money gambling or wagering.
  • Interactive Controls: Customizable virtual joystick, boost buttons, sensitivity settings, and haptic vibration feedback.

2. Information We Collect and Process

✓ Zero Personal Identity Tracking: Zig Snake does NOT collect, ask for, or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your real name, physical address, phone number, personal contacts, GPS location, or personal photos. You can play the game completely anonymously without signing up for any proprietary user account.

A. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All your in-game progress is kept entirely offline on your device using standard local web/Android persistent storage (Local Storage):

  • Current balance of virtual coins and gems.
  • Unlocked snake skins and visual arena themes.
  • High scores, match statistics, kill counts, and longest snake lengths.
  • Custom control layout coordinates and sensitivity preferences.
  • Audio settings (music volume, sound effects, and haptic vibration on/off).

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To provide Zig Snake as a free-to-play game and enable players to voluntarily earn bonus in-game coins, revives, and rewards, we integrate Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC).

Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personal device information through Google Play Services in accordance with Google's Privacy Policies:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID): Used by Google to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements, prevent ad fraud, and frequency-cap impressions.
  • Device & Network Identifiers: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, carrier, language settings, and general network connection type (Wi-Fi/Cellular).
  • Ad Interaction Data: Ad views, clicks, and rewarded video completion events (used to grant you your in-game reward).
Offline Respect & No Forced Spam: If you are offline or have no internet connection, Zig Snake detects your offline status immediately and disables ad loading without penalizing gameplay. You can continue playing offline uninterrupted.

To learn more about how Google handles user data and to control personalized ads on your device:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising and Privacy
  • You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID or Delete Advertising ID.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Family Friendly)

Zig Snake is a family-friendly game intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately, and we will delete it promptly.

6. Data Retention and Deletion Rights

You have full control over your data. To delete all saved progress:

  • Clear Data: Open Android Settings → Apps → Zig Snake → Storage → Clear Data.
  • Uninstall: Removing the app from your device automatically deletes all local files created by Zig Snake.

7. Security Safeguards

The game is compiled with the latest secure Android API Level, signed with a 2048-bit RSA production cryptographic certificate, and relies exclusively on certified Google Play Services.
