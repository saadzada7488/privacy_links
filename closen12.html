Privacy Policy for Closen 12

Package Name: com.mrhideo.closen | Effective Date: November 6, 2026
Overview: This Privacy Policy governs the use of Closen 12 (accessible via package ID com.mrhideo.closen), a closed-testing coordination platform designed for Android developers to complete Google Play Console's 12 testers & 14 days closed testing requirements collaboratively.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, maintain, and secure our mutual app-testing community services:

A. Personal Information & Authentication Data

  • Google Account Information: When signing in with Google via Android Credential Manager, we collect your verified email address, Google User ID (UID), and public display name.
  • Email/Password Accounts: If you register via email, we store your email address and securely hashed authentication credentials through Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile Data: Public username, testing stats, and participation badges.

B. In-App Messaging, Feedbacks & Proofs

  • Developer Feedback & In-App Reports: Content of feedback messages, bug reports, issue summaries, and ratings you submit to app developers.
  • Daily Testing Proof Submissions: Uploaded screenshots and testing logs verifying active participation in 14-day closed testing cycles.
  • Direct Chat & Group Communications: Tester group discussions, testing coordination requests, and notifications between app owners and community testers.

C. Application Listing Information

  • App titles, package IDs, store listing URLs, opt-in testing links, Google Groups links, and app icons provided by developers seeking testers.

D. Financial & Transaction Data (Google Play Billing)

  • When purchasing in-app virtual items (Snakes or Diamonds), transactions are processed exclusively through Google Play Billing.
  • We NEVER collect, store, or have access to your credit card, bank, or payment card numbers.
  • We only receive an encrypted purchaseToken, orderId, and product identifier from Google Play to verify and credit virtual items.

2. How We Use Collected Information

We use your information strictly for legitimate application functionality:

  • To connect developers with eligible community testers for Google Play Console closed testing.
  • To verify genuine 14-day daily testing participation via automated server timestamps and reviewer audits.
  • To deliver push notifications regarding test invites, feedback messages, daily reminders, and reward disbursements.
  • To maintain secure in-app virtual currency balances (Snakes & Diamonds) via server-side atomic transactions.
  • To prevent fraud, multiple account creation, and artificial review manipulation.

3. Android Permissions Required & Justification

Permission Type Purpose & Justification
INTERNET Normal Required to synchronize testing records, communicate with Firebase Firestore/Cloud Functions, and connect to Google Play Billing.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal Detects device connectivity to prevent failed cloud submissions and manage offline caching.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime Sends alerts when developers accept testing requests, when daily testing reminders fire, or when feedbacks are received.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE (SPECIAL_USE) Service Permits the testing countdown timer (app_testing_countdown) to accurately track required in-app testing duration without battery termination.
VIBRATE Normal Provides haptic feedback upon receiving test rewards and critical notifications.

4. Messaging & User Generated Content (UGC) Policy

Community Guidelines: Closen 12 facilitates communications between developers and testers. We strictly prohibit harassment, hate speech, spam, abusive language, or solicitation of fake or deceptive reviews.
  • Public Feedbacks: Constructive app feedback submitted by testers is shared directly with the respective app developer.
  • Reporting Inappropriate Content: Users can flag or report malicious apps, fraudulent links, or abusive messages. Reported content is reviewed promptly by administrators.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We integrate certified industry-standard third-party services that adhere to rigorous data privacy frameworks:

  • Google Play Services & Billing: For secure in-app payments and identity verification. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore: For encrypted user data storage, authentication state, and realtime synchronization. (Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • Firebase Cloud Functions: For authoritative server-side reward validation and purchase token verification.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

We believe in absolute user autonomy over their personal data:

In-App Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account, listed apps, testing history, and associated data at any time directly inside the app:

Go to: Profile → Settings → Delete Account

Upon initiating deletion:

  • Your Firebase Auth account is permanently deleted.
  • Your user profile, private balance records, and active testing sessions in Firestore are immediately erased.
  • All local Room database tables and preferences are scrubbed from your physical device.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is directed at software developers and QA testers aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will immediately delete it from our servers.

8. Security of Your Data

We implement industry-grade security measures including encrypted TLS/HTTPS transmission, zero-trust Firestore security rules, Cloud Functions token verification, and automated server timestamp validation to protect your data against unauthorized access or tampering.
