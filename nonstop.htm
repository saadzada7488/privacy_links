Official Privacy Policy

Non Stop Ball

Package Name: com.mrhideo.nonstopball
Effective Date: November 1, 2026
Developer: MrHideo

Thank you for playing Non Stop Ball! Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we do and do not collect, how third-party advertising functions, and how your data is protected.

1. Overview & Information Collection

Non Stop Ball is an arcade brick breaker mobile game. We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as full names, physical addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, or contact lists.

The game requires no user registration, login, or cloud accounts to play.

2. Game Data & Local Storage

Your gameplay progress is stored strictly locally on your device storage (via local storage / app preferences):

  • Unlocked Levels: Progress across all 600 arcade levels.
  • High Scores & Stars: High scores and star ratings earned.
  • Customization: Equipped and unlocked paddle models, colors, and skill charges.
  • Preferences: Audio toggles (Music, Sound), Screen Shake, and Vibration settings.

This data is never sent to external private servers. You can reset or erase it at any time in the in-game Settings menu or by clearing the app data in Android system settings.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

To offer Non Stop Ball for free, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. These include:

  • Banner Ads: Displayed at the bottom of menus and pause screens.
  • Interstitial Ads: Full-screen ads shown occasionally between rounds or retry attempts.
  • Rewarded Video Ads: Optional ads that players choose to watch to earn in-game skill charges.

Google AdMob and Google Play Services may process non-personal diagnostic data and device identifiers to serve appropriate ads and prevent fraud:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID)
  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version
  • IP address (used to infer coarse regional location for ad language/localization)
  • Ad interaction metrics (impressions, clicks)

For more details, review Google's official privacy documentation:

  • Google Privacy & Terms Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps
  • Google AdMob Privacy Information

4. Device Permissions Used

Non Stop Ball requests only the minimal technical permissions necessary:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required by Google AdMob to load ads and check connectivity.
  • VIBRATE: Used for tactile haptic feedback during paddle hits and brick breaks (can be turned off in Settings).

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Non Stop Ball is a family-friendly game suitable for all ages:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
  • Third-party ad requests adhere to Google's Families Policy and Child-Directed Treatment guidelines.
  • If a parent or guardian has any questions, please contact us immediately at the email below.

6. Data Security & Opt-Out

Since we do not maintain personal accounts on remote servers, your private data is not exposed to server-side leaks. You can manage or reset your advertising ID anytime via your Android device:

  • Go to device Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID.
